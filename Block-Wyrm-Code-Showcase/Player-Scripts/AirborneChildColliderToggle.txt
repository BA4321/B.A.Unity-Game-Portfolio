I during development i was faced with a problem which was basically this;
Player has a one big collider used for walking and one smaller collider inside the big collider to function as a playercore that instantly killed the player when player was crushed, sometimes despite being inside a block the insta kill core wouldn't work and player would get stuck. 

To fix this i thought a lot about the problem and tested few things,
First i put the instakill collider at players head and this resulted in a very hard and unforgiving experience, even with save mechanic player would constantly get teleported up.

Second i made the walking collider small and put the instakill collider on top of the smaller walking collider, so that player now has a better more forgiving grace period. There were two problems, player could walk into 1 block gaps and jumping and hitting a falling block meant death. 
Unfortunately simple solution of making it so instakill only works when player is grounded didn't work reliably and could still end with player stuck inside blocks.
For that i made a new collider that gets activated when player is not grounded that covers the instakill collider to stop "bonk head while jumping = instant death"
And to make the player not be able to walk into 1 block gaps, i simply made walking collider slightly larger than 1 block.
