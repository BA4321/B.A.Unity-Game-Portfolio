I needed a very customizable camera follow that;
Doesn't shake with micro movements,
When player goes up, goes after player with a normal speed,
When player goes down a little, goes after player with high speed,
When pplayer goes down a lot(aka falling off the map), it goes after player very slowly, 
This slowness is there because outofbounds player killer box is attached to camera and if camera follows player's fall player will never reach the outofboundsplayerkiller. 

This player killer follows player via following camera because at certain heights when player fell down it may take a long while for player to reach ground, and making a script thats used to make killer seperately chase after player seemed needlessly complex for little reward, so i instead focused on camera following to get this custom killzone positioning and make camera more responsive.
