## Targeting and physical movement

- **TARGET-01 — Identity and eligibility.** Enemy means a different non-null combatant with a different faction. Faction is a serialized field, readable without Awake. It changes only through `SetFaction`, which succeeds only while the GameObject is inactive in the hierarchy; an active unit logs an error, keeps its faction and gets false. Enemy relations therefore cannot change while a unit can fight (world-map callers: `WM-RECRUIT-01`, `WM-FACTION-01`). Targetability requires active root/combatant/health, living health and active motor outside Disabled/Dead. Dead/Disabled owners clear their target; Knockback keeps target maintenance.
- **TARGET-02 — Selection.** Query the enemy layer mask within the authored search radius using reusable storage (triggers included); resolve combatant locally or in parents and choose nearest squared distance among returned valid enemies. Searches are interval-based, staggered on enable; invalidating an assigned target triggers immediate replacement search. Unlike Area collection, no grow-and-retry completeness guarantee is established for this targeting buffer. **Mask binding.** Awake copies the serialized `EnemyLayerMask` into the live query filter once. `SetEnemyLayerMask` writes the field and, once the query exists (after Awake), refreshes the live filter too, because Awake does not run again on re-enable. Battle deployment rebinds it at every deployment to the enemy side's body layer only (`SIDE-02`); the prefab's saved mask is not authoritative in battle. [WM4: I08 binding test (masks correct; a rebind after Awake refreshes the live filter); I10 units get the right masks]
- **MOVE-01 — Momentum and steering.** Each moving fixed step clamps clean forward speed to effective maximum, then advances toward it using effective acceleration. A lower limit applies even with zero acceleration; raising the limit grants no instant speed. Explicit speed set/multiply also clamps. Ordinary movement faces the live target and applies forward velocity. Knockback suppresses normal and override steering but continues rebuilding forward momentum and combines forward/Knockback velocities; expiration returns Seeking. **Destination override (Seeking only).** While the same-root `UnitDestinationOverride2D` holds an override, the motor ignores the target: velocity is the direction to the destination × applied speed, and facing turns toward that direction at effective turn speed. Within the arrive distance (0.05 units in source) velocity is zero while momentum keeps building. Because velocity follows the destination direction rather than facing, movement and facing briefly disagree while the unit turns; when the override ends, ordinary steering resumes from the current facing, so velocity snaps to facing. [WM4: I09 real units walk in from their edges in both perimeter modes; ejected units walk back in (I10)]
- **MOVE-02 — Snapshot reconciliation.** TryReconcileForwardSpeedLimit clamps without advancing momentum or decaying Knockback, rebuilding velocity from facing × (clean speed + stored Sidestep bonus), plus separate Knockback while in that state. In Seeking with an active destination override the direction is the destination direction instead of facing (zero within the arrive distance), matching `MOVE-01`. Stat changes do not use modifier events to immediately rewrite velocity. [override branch WM4-S]
- **MOVE-03 — State and intent.** Disabled/Dead stop Rigidbody motion; SetDisabled cannot exit Dead. MarkDead stops motion before and after state callbacks. CanReadMovement requires enabled motor, usable dependencies and neither Disabled nor Dead. Motor disable/Disabled/Dead clears its bonus channel. After momentum/Knockback handling and the turn-speed read, a Seeking motor uses exactly one source, in this order: destination override (`MOVE-01`) → Sidestep intent → ordinary Seeking. Under an override Sidestep is not consulted. Otherwise the motor asks Sidestep for intent using applied speed and fixed delta; returned velocity drives the body, while effective turn speed × returned multiplier faces the live target. Sidestep never reads runtime stats/controller directly. A destination-override reference on another GameObject fails validation and disables the motor. [WM4: I09/I10 probe "Sidestep under override" 0]
- **MOVE-04 — Deployment reset.** `TryPrepareForDeployment(position, facing, out error)` is for parked units only. It refuses a GameObject active in the hierarchy, a Dead motor, a missing definition, and a non-finite position or facing or a zero facing. On success it sets the transform pose (facing along the given direction), clears Knockback and the motor's own Sidestep bonus channel, sets clean speed to the authored initial speed (clamped by the next momentum step), changes state to Seeking (normally unheard: a parked Sidestep is unsubscribed) and marks a pending reset. The next OnEnable consumes it: the Rigidbody pose is copied from the transform and its velocity stopped. It never writes Sidestep session, cooldown or bonus bookkeeping (Roadmap §2.9; deactivation already cancelled any session, `SIDESTEP-LIFE-02`). It uses no Unity message, so it is safe on a never-awakened unit. Whether Awake's own initial-speed setup (`INIT-01`) runs on that first activation is not re-verified here; either way the next momentum step clamps clean speed to the effective maximum. Caller: `BATTLE-SPAWN-02`. [WM4: units deploy and walk in (I09, I10); refusal paths WM4-S]

## Sidestep lifecycle pipeline

- **SIDESTEP-GATE-01 — Continuous band eligibility.** Every fixed step inside the inclusive squared min/max distance band is an opportunity; no band crossing is needed and rejected opportunities are not queued. Target loss/replacement discards observed identity but preserves the unit-wide retry cooldown.
- **SIDESTEP-GATE-02 — Ordered gates and retry.** Enabled configuration → expired retry cooldown → no active/pending/ending lifecycle → enabled Seeking motor → no active destination override → still-current target, enabled targeting and targetable target → chance roll. Pre-roll rejection, including the override gate, starts no cooldown. At most one roll per step. Chance failure starts cooldown; every started lifecycle completion/cancellation, including path or bonus failure, starts cooldown through cleanup. Positive retry cooldown spaces repeated opportunities; zero allows a roll each eligible step, making eventual success increasingly likely for nonzero chance. The override reference is optional; absent, this gate always passes. [override gate WM4: I09/I10 probe 0; Sidesteps still occur once units are inside (I09)]
- **SIDESTEP-PATH-01 — Geometry and Wall preflight.** Snapshot start and target endpoint once as opposite ends of a fixed half-circle; reject non-finite/unusable geometry. Randomly prefer CW/CCW, try the opposite if blocked. Clearance covers the full body with start overlap and conservative chord circle casts including curvature/numeric padding, against `Wall` and, when the project has the layer, `Perimeter` (both resolved once at initialization); queries exclude triggers and reuse buffers. Later target movement never rebuilds the arc. Exact subdivision count/padding belongs to source. [Perimeter in the preflight mask WM4-S]
- **SIDESTEP-PATH-02 — Intent and progress.** Progress uses supplied applied speed and fixed delta against the reference arc, not realized Rigidbody displacement; displacement does not steer back onto that arc. Deliver at most one movement interval per fixed step and the final interval exactly once. Live target remains the facing target.
- **SIDESTEP-LIFE-01 — Start/end.** Become active only after valid path, Wall clearance and motor bonus application. Compute/store a fixed bonus from clean speed once at start. Centralized reentrancy-safe cleanup marks inactive, removes the exact stored bonus if applied, invalidates path, records end reason and starts cooldown. The motor owns the applied channel; lifecycle owns bookkeeping.
- **SIDESTEP-LIFE-02 — Interruptions.** Cancel on Knockback/Disabled/Dead StateChanged, a destination override starting or changing reason (its `Changed` while `HasOverride`; an override ending does not cancel), unavailable/disabled targeting or motor, lost/replaced/untargetable target, non-Seeking state, an active override found by session validation (each fixed step and before movement intent), invalid path, component disable or live Wall or Perimeter solid enter/stay. OnDisable unsubscribes from the motor and the override (the exact instance it subscribed to), cancels and discards observed identity. Sidestep subscribes to motor state and override changes, not directly to health/death. [override cancellation WM4: probe 0 (I09, I10); Perimeter contact WM4-S]
- **SIDESTEP-CLASH-01 — Accepted preparation.** Participant delegates PrepareForAcceptedClash to same-root Sidestep. Active session cancels normally, removing bonus and requesting invalidation/cooldown, then motor rebuilds clean velocity from current facing. Inactive session is a no-op. Director's paired ordering is `CLASH-07`–`CLASH-09`.
- **SIDESTEP-ORDER-01 — Fixed coordinator.** Validate non-Clash interruptions → finish prior delivered final interval → reconcile observed target/in-band status → evaluate one opportunity → reset per-step intent/cache for later motor consumption. All Unity messages are on UnitSidestep2D.cs.

## Contract cards

### UnitCombatant2D [V7, WM2]
Owns: faction identity; targetability facade.  
API: Faction, Health, WorldPosition, MajorState, CanBeTargeted, IsEnemyOf; `SetFaction(FactionId) → bool` (inactive-in-hierarchy only; safe before Awake).  
Events: none documented.  
Invariants: `TARGET-01`.  
Never owns: target selection, health, movement state, Sidestep or progression.

### UnitTargeting2D [B, WM4]
Owns: current target, search deadline, reusable query/filter state, serialized enemy mask.  
API: CurrentTarget, NextSearchTime; `EnemyLayerMask` (serialized, readable without Awake), `IsQueryInitialized`, `ActiveQueryMask` (0 before Awake), `SetEnemyLayerMask(LayerMask)` (safe on parked never-awakened units; refreshes the live filter after Awake).  
Events: none documented.  
Invariants: `TARGET-01`, `TARGET-02`; `SIDE-02`.  
Never owns: faction, movement, Sidestep eligibility or damage.

### UnitMotor2D [F,B, WM4]
Owns: major state, clean speed, applied bonus, Knockback, Rigidbody writes, destination-override steering, pending deployment reset.  
API: BeginKnockback, Set/MultiplyForwardSpeed, TryApply/RemoveSidestepSpeedBonus, RebuildCleanForwardVelocityFromCurrentFacing, TryReconcileForwardSpeedLimit, SetDisabled, MarkDead, CanReadMovement; `TryPrepareForDeployment(Vector2 position, Vector2 facingDirection, out string error) → bool`; `DestinationOverride`, `HasDestinationOverride`, CurrentState, CurrentPosition, CurrentVelocity, FacingDirection.  
Events: StateChanged → enabled Sidestep interruption handler.  
Invariants: `MOVE-01`–`MOVE-04`; `ARENA-DEST-01`.  
Never owns: targeting, effective stats, Sidestep path/lifecycle/cooldown, health or death lifecycle, the override's value or when it is set (arena).

### UnitSidestep2D [SID, WM4]
Owns: one session, observed target, unit-wide cooldown, path/Wall state and bonus bookkeeping.  
API: TryConsumeSidestepMovementIntent; PrepareForAcceptedClash; `IsSidestepActive` (read-only diagnostic: an active, not ending, session; read by the battle probe).  
Events: none emitted; subscribes to motor StateChanged and the destination override's `Changed` while enabled.  
Invariants: `SIDESTEP-GATE-01`, `SIDESTEP-GATE-02`, `SIDESTEP-PATH-01`, `SIDESTEP-PATH-02`, `SIDESTEP-LIFE-01`, `SIDESTEP-LIFE-02`, `SIDESTEP-CLASH-01`, `SIDESTEP-ORDER-01`.  
Never owns: target selection, major state, Rigidbody execution, effective stats or paired transaction order.
